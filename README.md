# Awesome-Subscription-Based-Social-Media-Platform

## Top Subscription-Based Social Media Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Creator Monetization, Membership Management & Direct Fan Support*  

**Last updated: March 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Subscription-Based Social Media Platforms**. These tools enable creators to monetize content through recurring memberships, paywalled posts, exclusive community access, and direct fan payments — giving creators an alternative to ad-driven platforms where algorithmic reach dictates income.



**Examples** include OnlyFans, Patreon, Substack, Fanvue, Fansly, Memberful, Locals, Kajabi, Fourthwall, and Buy Me a Coffee (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom membership logic, and transparent revenue tracking — ideal for creators prioritizing data sovereignty, developers building creator platforms, and anyone avoiding platform fees.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[OnlyFans](https://onlyfans.com/)**  

  Subscription-based content platform with 305M+ registered users (2023). Creators charge monthly subscriptions for exclusive content, with tipping for unlocking additional posts. Platform takes 20% commission on all earnings . Primarily known for adult content but also hosts art, fitness, and music creators.



- **[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/)**  

  Leading membership platform for creators. Since August 2025, new pages pay a flat 10% on membership income plus payment processing fees . Older plans: Pro at 8%, Premium at 12%, plus 5% on merchandise sales . Features multiple subscription tiers, Discord integration, and community posts .



- **[Substack](https://substack.com/)**  

  Newsletter and subscription publishing platform for writers and journalists. Creators charge for premium subscriptions to newsletters and podcasts. Platform takes 10% of subscription revenue plus Stripe fees. Strong focus on email list ownership .



- **[Fanvue](https://fanvue.com/)**  

  Subscription social media platform with AI creator tools, messaging, and content paywalls.



- **[Fansly](https://fansly.com/)**  

  Subscription-based content platform with creator-friendly policies and competitive commission structure.



- **[Memberful](https://memberful.com/)**  

  Membership plugin that integrates with existing websites (WordPress, Squarespace) for subscriptions, gated content, and member management.



- **[Locals](https://locals.com/)**  

  Community and subscription platform founded by Dave Rubin. Creators host paid communities with posts, videos, and live streams.



- **[Kajabi](https://kajabi.com/)**  

  All-in-one creator platform for courses, memberships, podcasts, and communities with built-in marketing and payments.



- **[Fourthwall](https://fourthwall.com/)**  

  Creator commerce platform for merch, digital products, memberships, and exclusive content with print-on-demand fulfillment.



- **[Buy Me a Coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/)**  

  Simple tipping and membership platform with 5% platform fee plus payment processing . Popular for one-time donations and small recurring support.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Patron](https://github.com/patroninc/patron)**  

  Open-source Patreon alternative with **lifetime 1% fees** designed for creators publishing sequential content like webcomics, serialized novels, podcasts, and ongoing series. Features **rolling paywalls** where new content starts gated to supporters and automatically becomes free over time based on customizable schedules . Rust backend, React frontend, TypeScript SDK, and self-hostable for complete control .



- **[Open Creator Rails](https://github.com/ChainSafe/open-creator-rails)**  

  Open-source blockchain paywall and subscription stack built under contract for the Ethereum Foundation. Enables creators to run **gated content directly on their own landing pages**, sidestepping platform monetization thresholds entirely . Onchain access control via smart contracts, instant wallet-to-creator transactions, and self-hosted deployment. Includes demonstration "Pet Shop" interface with locked premium content .



- **[Cloud Patron](https://github.com/cloudpatron/cloudpatron)**  

  Open-source Patreon alternative providing subscription-based funding and content gating for independent creators . 247 GitHub stars.



- **[Mirlo](https://github.com/funmusicplace/mirlo)**  

  Open-source audio distribution and patronage platform designed for musicians and audio creators to distribute work and receive direct support from listeners . 154 GitHub stars.



- **[Crikket](https://railway.com/deploy/crikket--crikket)**  

  Open-source platform for building and managing creator memberships, digital products, communities, and subscription-based businesses. Provides authentication, payments, media storage, member management, and modern web interface. Self-hostable alternative to Patreon, Gumroad, or Circle . Deployable via Railway with PostgreSQL and S3-compatible storage .



- **[Open Payment Host](https://github.com/abishekmuthian/open-payment-host)**  

  Self-hosted payment and subscription host supporting **Stripe, Square, PayPal, and Razorpay** with webhook integration. Handles subscription creation, cancellation, and payment tracking. Go-based with Docker support .



- **[Markdown Ninja](https://github.com/skerkour/markdown-ninja)**  

  Markdown-first CMS for bloggers, minimalists, and startups. Open-source alternative to Medium, Mailchimp, and **Substack** . Publish Markdown-based websites and newsletters with one command. AGPL-3.0 licensed .



- **[Fountain](https://github.com/fountain-ink/app)**  

  Decentralized blogging platform positioned as a web3 alternative to Medium, Substack, and Mirror. Built on Lens Protocol for decentralized social, Grove for decentralized storage, with Listmonk for mailing lists. AGPL-3.0 licensed .



- **[Createon](https://github.com/opd-ai/createon)**  

  Self-hosted Patreon alternative enabling creators to monetize content using **cryptocurrency payments** (Bitcoin and Monero). Multiple subscription tiers, Markdown content support, tier-restricted access, automated payment verification, and flat-file architecture with no database required. Go-based with CLI for creator management .



- **[Mila](https://interoperable-europe.ec.europa.eu/eu-oss-catalogue/solutions/mila)**  

  Free and open-source membership management tool for clubs and associations. Features member data management, fee tracking, custom fields, OIDC single sign-on, member self-service, and online applications. AGPL-3.0 licensed .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core)** — Open-source CRM with membership management, contribution tracking, and recurring payment support for nonprofits and associations.

- **[Ghost](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost)** — Open-source publishing platform with native memberships, subscriptions, and newsletters. Built-in Stripe integration for paid tiers.

- **[Listmonk](https://github.com/knadh/listmonk)** — Open-source newsletter and mailing list manager for creator audience communications.

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)** — Open-source ERP with subscription billing, CRM, and accounting modules adaptable for creator businesses.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Patron** for sequential content monetization with rolling paywalls, **Open Creator Rails** for blockchain-based direct creator payments, **Crikket** for full membership and digital product management, **Open Payment Host** for multi-gateway subscription billing, and **Ghost** for publishing with native memberships to build a complete self-hosted creator monetization stack.
